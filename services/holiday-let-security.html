Service · Holiday-let security

Security for a holiday let, without watching your guests.

Cameras on entry points and the property boundary, not inside guest space. Alerts that tell the owner what matters between changeovers, without turning into a surveillance problem for paying guests.

What's included

  • Exterior-only camera placement as standard: entrances, driveway, garden boundary
  • Clear signage where a camera's coverage means guests should be told it is present
  • Remote access for the owner or management team, with changeover alerts if useful
  • Smart lock and sensor options for a keyless changeover, where wanted

Honest pricing

Fixed price confirmed after a £49 survey (credited against the job) or from clear photos for straightforward jobs. Bands reflect access, property type and how much configuration is involved.

Holiday lets around Sandwich and the coast

Sandwich and the Bay have a busy seasonal changeover market. Owners want to know a property is secure between bookings without making guests feel watched, that balance drives every placement decision on a let.

Where we stop

We will not fit a camera inside a guest-occupied room or a bathroom under any circumstance, and would say no to that request. Exterior and communal-entrance cameras only, and disclosed to guests before they book where your listing platform or terms require it.
