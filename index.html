Why local matters

East Kent throws up its own camera problems.

Sandwich's historic centre is close-packed flint and timber-framed buildings, cable runs and fixings need care that a generic national installer will not bother with. Out along Sandwich Bay and the Stour, footpaths and public rights of way run close to garden boundaries, which matters more than most people realise once a doorbell camera is pointed at the front gate.

Second homes and holiday lets are common along this stretch of coast, so remote-friendly setups (an app that actually tells you something useful, alerts that do not cry wolf every time a fox crosses the drive) matter more here than in a dense city street. We size and site systems for that reality, not a generic checklist.

Common questions

Five things people ask before booking.

Do you cover Deal and Dover as well as Sandwich?

Yes. Sandwich is home base, and the round covers Deal, Walmer, Dover, Ash, Wingham, Woodnesborough, Eastry and the villages between. Further out along the coast is fine on request, travel just gets factored into the quote.

Will my doorbell camera pick up my neighbour's garden or the street?

Quite possibly, most doorbells have a wide field of view and doorsteps sit close to a boundary or a footpath. We angle the camera to cover your own porch and approach as tightly as the job allows, and talk you through the privacy and ICO points that apply once a camera looks past your own boundary. See the legislation and compliance guide for the detail.

Is this supply-and-fit only, or can you set up monitoring too?

Both. Most jobs are supply, install and app set-up, with you checking the footage yourself. If you want a monitored service with alerts or dispatch, we can connect you to an accredited monitoring partner, priced on the pricing page.

What if there's no broadband where the camera needs to go?

A 4G camera runs on a mobile data SIM instead of your home Wi-Fi, useful for gates, outbuildings, land and temporary sites. Data plan is billed separately by the network, we handle the hardware and setup.

Do you install alarm systems, or just cameras?

We fit smart door, window and motion sensors that push alerts to your phone, and can wire that into a monitored service through our monitoring partner. For a full monitored intruder alarm with guard response as the primary system, that is a specialist alarm company's job and we will say so rather than sell you something we do not do well.
