CT13 · CT14 · CT16 · CT17

Where we work.

Sandwich is home base. The round runs out through the estuary and Nailbourne villages, and along the coast to Deal and Dover. Three different settings, three different sets of camera and Wi-Fi problems.

Sandwich and the estuary villages (CT13)

The core round. Sandwich's historic centre is close-packed flint, brick and timber frame around the Guildhall and the old town walls, cable runs and external fixings need a lighter touch here than on a modern estate. Out towards Sandwich Bay, footpaths and golf courses (Royal St George's and the neighbouring links) sit close to garden boundaries, which matters for doorbell and camera placement, not just for Wi-Fi range.

  • Sandwich town, historic centre, mixed housing stock close to the Guildhall and the River Stour.
  • Sandwich Bay, coastal properties and second homes near the estate and golf courses, higher weather exposure on hardware.
  • Woodnesborough, village on the edge of town, mixed older and newer housing.
  • Eastry, larger village a short drive out, mixed stock and generous gardens.
  • Worth, small village between Sandwich and Deal, rural boundaries and outbuildings common.

Ash, Wingham and the Nailbourne villages (CT3)

Rolling countryside north-west of Sandwich, sheltered from the direct coastal weather but with the same long-garden and outbuilding pattern that makes Wi-Fi reach and solar orientation worth checking properly rather than assuming.

  • Ash, the largest of this group, mixed housing and a working village centre.
  • Wingham, historic village with a long high street, older housing stock.
  • Preston, smaller village near Wingham, rural boundaries.
  • Staple, small village, mostly rural properties and outbuildings.

Deal and Dover (CT14, CT16–17)

Market towns further down the coast. Both are covered as a standard part of the round, slightly further out than the Sandwich core, so travel time can show in the quote on smaller jobs.

  • Deal and Walmer, seafront and town properties, similar coastal exposure considerations to Sandwich Bay.
  • Dover, town and surrounding villages, covered on request.

Not sure if we cover you?

Our standard round runs from Sandwich out to roughly Deal and Dover, comfortably covering CT13, CT3, CT14, and CT16–17. Anything further out (towards Canterbury or Folkestone) we will take on request, travel time will show in the quote. If you are inside the area above, we are your local installer.
